# Data handling and redistribution

The provided academic notebook names Instacart 2017. Dataset reference: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/instacart-market-basket-analysis/data . The files are a partial extract with deliberate-looking missing cells, but their modification history is unknown; corruption is observed, its cause is not established.

No licence, download receipt, source extraction script or redistribution permission accompanied the copied CSVs. A public competition page is not sufficient evidence of unrestricted redistribution. No new licence is assigned to these records or to the original team's academic work. Verify the applicable terms and contributor permissions before publishing those materials.

The raw CSVs (roughly 270 MiB) and derived real cohort remain local and are ignored by Git. Original notebooks may contain saved tables and figures derived from real records; review them before publishing even though they are small enough for Git. Real-cohort screenshots show aggregate results and searched product names; they contain no credentials or identified persons. The stored evaluation report contains aggregate counts and performance, not customer records.

The public cloud path is already operational with data generated by `scripts/prepare_data.py`: 650 fictional customer identifiers, 24 generic grocery products and a fixed seed. No original records, taxonomy files or product catalogue are used by that generator. A synthetic co-purchase tendency is explicitly introduced for demonstration. Its predictive performance cannot establish real-world validity.

Real preparation excludes invalid context orders (129 source records), then samples 3,000 user IDs with seed 42, streams matching lines, excludes 16 corrupted/non-contiguous cohort baskets, and retains orders with observed lines. This yields 2,948 observed customers and 20,256 observed baskets. Source hashes and exact cohort exclusions are in the ignored `data/local/manifest.json`. This cleaning introduces a population restriction that is disclosed, not silently repaired by fabricating missing targets.

Elapsed time is missing structurally on first orders. Exploratory analyses retain that distinction. First-order nulls are permitted by validation; non-first-order nulls are rejected. Null IDs, conflicting keys, binary-target violations and broken foreign keys fail with an actionable error. Extreme valid basket sizes are not trimmed. The maximum source cart position is 127; the data contain no prices from which to infer financial outliers.
